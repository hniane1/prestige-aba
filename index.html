About Us

Dedicated to Every Child's Unique Journey

At Prestige ABA Therapy LLC, we believe every child has the capacity for growth and achievement. Our team of Board Certified Behavior Analysts and skilled technicians delivers individualized, evidence-based interventions across home, school, and community settings.

Founded and led by Kadiatou Diallo, MS, LBS, BCBA — with over 10 years in the field — we combine clinical excellence with genuine compassion to support children and families across Coatesville, PA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is a scientifically validated therapy that improves socially significant behaviors using principles of learning. It helps children develop communication, social, self-care, and adaptive skills while reducing behaviors that interfere with learning and daily life.
Source: Autism Speaks — Applied Behavior Analysis
We serve children and young adults ages 2–21 who may have autism spectrum disorder, developmental delays, behavioral challenges, or other conditions that affect daily functioning. Every child is evaluated individually to determine the most appropriate services.
Source: NIH / StatPearls — Applied Behavior Analysis
Our comprehensive assessment is typically completed within 15 days of intake. This includes a functional behavior assessment, skill evaluation, and family interview, resulting in a detailed treatment plan tailored to your child.
Yes. We are in-network with Aetna, Geisinger Health Plan, and Highmark of Pennsylvania. Pennsylvania's autism-insurance law (Act 62) requires many state-regulated plans to cover medically necessary ABA services.
